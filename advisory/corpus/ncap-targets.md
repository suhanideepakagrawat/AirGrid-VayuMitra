## What NCAP is

The National Clean Air Programme is India's national framework for reducing particulate
pollution in cities that persistently exceed the National Ambient Air Quality Standards.
It funds city-level clean air action plans and the expansion of the ambient monitoring
network.

## Targets

The programme launched with a target of a 20 to 30 percent reduction in PM10 and PM2.5
concentrations by 2024 against a 2017 baseline. The target was subsequently revised to a
40 percent reduction in particulate concentration, or attainment of the National Ambient
Air Quality Standards, by 2025-26.

## Non-attainment cities

Cities are identified as non-attainment where ambient concentrations exceeded the
National Ambient Air Quality Standards over a five-year period. Each such city prepares a
city-specific action plan covering road dust, vehicular emissions, industrial emissions,
construction and demolition waste, and biomass burning.

## Monitoring and accountability

NCAP funds Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Stations and manual monitoring, with
performance linked to measurable reduction in particulate concentration. The programme
also emphasises source apportionment studies and emission inventories as the basis for
targeted action, rather than uniform city-wide measures.
